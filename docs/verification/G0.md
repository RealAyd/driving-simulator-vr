## Issues found and fixed during G0

1. Holding the accelerator against an obstacle produced five collision events instead of one. Contacts are now latched until the car moves 0.3 m clear.
2. Visual review (not caught by tests): the road showed through the car's door and footwell, and the windscreen header was oversized. Added a full-width dashboard, door panels, floor, roof lining and footwell bulkhead.

## Performance

Headless SwiftShader (software rendering, no GPU) reported 8–14 fps. **This is not representative** of any real device and must not be read as a desktop, phone or headset result. No real-device measurement has been made yet.

## Known limitations

- Placeholder geometry: boxes for houses, a primitive cockpit. Two small slivers remain visible at the bottom corners of the driver view.
- No mirrors (G1), other road users or exercises (G2), or assessment.
- No reverse gear; the car stops at obstacles rather than reacting physically.
- Touch controls exist for the phone profile but are untested on a real phone.
- VR: code path only; see `docs/xr-feasibility.md`. No headset test has been done.
- The CI workflow (`.github/workflows/sim-ci.yml`) has not run yet; it runs on the next push if the repository allows Actions.
- No preview hosting is configured. GitHub Pages serves the production site from `main`, so deploying the preview there would replace it. A separate preview host is a decision for G5.
