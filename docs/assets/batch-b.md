# Asset Batch B (corrected cars, QA v0.3): integration (2026-10-19)

Pack: `Driving_Practice_Batch_B_Corrected_Complete.zip`, supplied by the project owner. Register entry:
`driving-practice-batch-b-cars` in `content/assets/register.json`. The pack README flagged that the
source archive had no licence terms; on 2026-10-19 the project owner confirmed that the licence allows
use in the published app and in this public repository (models mostly generated by the owner).

Drives get a mix: mostly hatchbacks and saloons, some large saloons, occasionally a pickup. The existing test
checks that no drive car touches a house, wall, hedge or another car.

* `tools/assets/build-batch-b-cars.sh` rebuilds them (`ONLY=parked` builds just the parked-only pair) (weld, dedup, prune, simplify, quantise; parked
  versions through `tools/assets/simplify.mjs`).
* Body paint is one material (`Body`), recoloured per road user as before (the hatchback's roof,
  `Body.01`, keeps its colour). Road users are scaled to their collision length, as before.
* Wheels: `WheelSpin_2s` turns the 0.32 m wheels four times (8.04 m of travel); it is held paused and
  turned by the distance each road user has moved (`CarModel.roll`), so the wheels match the speed.
* Size: the models are drawn larger than the cars they show (the hatchback is 4.55 × 2.16 × 1.50 m with
  mirrors; a real compact hatchback is about 4.0 m long). Road users are scaled to their 4.3 m collision length;
  parked copies are scaled to 4.0 m (hatchback: 4.0 × 1.90 × 1.32 m) and 4.6 m (saloon: 4.6 × 1.81 × 1.37 m), and
  their collision boxes match. Kerbside cars stand 0.12 m from the kerb (`batch-b/parked-moving-off-forward.png`).
* Kerbside parked cars (2026-10-20): the wider cars come about 0.36 m further into the road than the narrow
  Quaternius car they replaced on the right-hand side of the Moving off street. The purple car's route was moved
  0.4 m towards the centre where it passes them (`content/scenarios/moving-off-safely.json`), keeping the tested
  0.2 m clearance. Nothing else in the scenario changed.
* If a model does not load, road users fall back to the other model, then to simple shapes.

Cost, phone profile, main view without mirrors (headless software rendering), against Batch A alone:
Brentwood 58 → 76 draw calls, +4k triangles; Kings Road 71 → 71, +7k; practice zebra 73 → 73, +7.6k.
Downloads +405 KB.

## Not used

* Sports, super and futuristic models (Bugatti-, Porsche-, Nissan-, Toyota-like, Cybertruck-like): not
  everyday Brentwood traffic, and heavier.
* Car 01 variants (`uk-traffic-car-01_*`): 15,598 triangles with 1024 px textures, about 956 KB each.
* Braking variants: brake lights belong to the simulator's road-user logic (not yet modelled).
* Cyclist QA files: the cyclist with contact pose is 4.6 MB (about 58,000 vertices). The drawn cyclist stays.
