# Brentwood pilot: five planned sets

Draft for the owner's review (2 November 2026). Each set follows part of the journeys reported from and back to
Brentwood test centre (89 Warley Hill), guided by the route screenshots and the two reported tests. Locations come from
OpenStreetMap (31 October 2026); the screenshots guide coverage only, never lane markings or priority.

Status: **ready** (layout from map data, nothing that changes the answer unknown), **check** (one named detail to
confirm before capture), **blocked** (needs facts the map does not have). Two finished samples: S1 and S2
(`samples/`).

Not every set reaches eight without forcing weak or repetitive situations: set D has six, and three others are
marked as optional.
