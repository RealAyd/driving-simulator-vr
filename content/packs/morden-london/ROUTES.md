# Morden (London) decision routes

Two "Decisions only" practice routes from the Morden test centre at 10 Tudor Drive, Morden SM4 4PE (gov.uk centre
finder, checked 15 November 2026; the map's "Driving Test Centre" point is on the centre's service road by the
Tudor Drive / Epsom Road lights). Built from OpenStreetMap data and the published evidence in
`content/sources/pass2/morden-london-evidence.json`, November 2026. Practice routes, not confirmed test routes. How
they were chosen and built: `HOW_WE_BUILT_IT.md`. The format: `../ROUTE_FORMAT.md`.

**Start and finish.** The centre is on a service road behind the Tudor Drive shops: its west end joins Tudor Drive 30
m short of the lights, its east end joins Epsom Road (the A24) just north of them. Route 1 leaves by the Tudor Drive
end and comes back in from Epsom Road; route 2 leaves by the Tudor Drive end and finishes on Tudor Drive at the way
out. *To confirm: which end examiners use, and whether the way in from Epsom Road is used.*

## Route 1: Sutton Common Road, Forest Road and Reigate Avenue (scene `morden-route-1`, 3.7 km)

Out of the centre, left on to Tudor Drive, ahead at the lights into Sutton Common Road, left at the Forest Road
mini-roundabout, Forest Road (pulling up and moving off), the Love Lane mini-roundabout into Glastonbury Road, right
on to Reigate Avenue, right at the lights into Sutton Common Road, Woodstock Avenue, Elm Road West, left on to Epsom

**Overlap**: 3.2 km of 3.8 km (85%) on roads in the evidence file (Tudor Drive 1.85 km, Grand Drive 0.47, Hillcross
Avenue 0.46, Cannon Hill Lane 0.33, the Beverley roundabout); 98% counting Churston Drive (0.5 km), named in the

junctions the source's route lists name (4, 8).

**The two routes together**: they leave in different directions (route 1 south-east through the lights, route 2
north-west up Tudor Drive) and share only the centre's way out. 16 different scenarios. Of the five known junctions
in the evidence file that suit a scenario, three are used (Tudor Drive / Epsom Road lights, Beverley roundabout,
Forest Road mini-roundabouts); Rosehill Roundabout and Shannon Corner are not (below).

## Found on the map

- **Rosehill Roundabout** (the only *confirmed* junction) is a signal-controlled gyratory of one-way pieces with
  lights on the ring. The scene builder does not recognise it as a roundabout, and roads crossing it (and the Green
  Lane dual carriageway, at Love Lane and Glastonbury Road) cross mid-way without a junction the planner or the
  traffic can use. Left out until the engine can model it; Green Lane's east side with it.
- **Shannon Corner** is a grade-separated A3 junction 2.7 km west: outside a 2.4 km scene with the centre in it.
- **The Beverley roundabout** has a small mapped building on its island; the engine's roundabout finder rejects a ring
  round a building, so the building tag was dropped in the downloaded data (it is not drawn).
- **The Tudor Drive approach to the lights** had no stop line in the scene (its signal node sits at the end of a way);
  the signal was moved 5 m back on to the approach in the downloaded data.
- **The B278 north of Green Lane's dual carriageway** has unnamed short ways the planner will not route through;
  they were named Green Lane in the downloaded data (route 1 no longer uses them, but the scene keeps the fix).
