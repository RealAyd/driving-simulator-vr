# Loughborough decision routes

Two "Decisions only" practice routes from the Loughborough test centre at the Ark Business Centre, Gordon Road,
Loughborough LE11 1JP, by the station (the evidence file's address, checked 15 November 2026 from three listings and
DVSA open data; gov.uk was not reachable; the map's "Loughborough Driving Test Centre" point is on the service loop at
the end of Gordon Road). Built from OpenStreetMap data and the published evidence in
`content/sources/pass2/loughborough-evidence.json`, November 2026. Practice routes, not confirmed test routes. How
they were chosen and built: `HOW_WE_BUILT_IT.md`. The format: `../ROUTE_FORMAT.md`.

**Start and finish.** Both start on the centre's service loop facing its way out to Gordon Road, and finish on
Gordon Road at the way in. Gordon Road meets Station Boulevard (the A6004 between the Meadow Lane lights and the
station) at a plain T junction. Route 1 turns right out of it, towards the station; route 2 turns left, towards the
Meadow Lane lights. *To confirm: how examiners leave and come back; the evidence predates the move to Gordon Road.*

## Why route 2 cannot reach 3 known junctions

Every known junction in the evidence is a roundabout, recorded at the position of a road rather than of the junction.
Within a scene that also holds the centre, three can be placed on a roundabout (Forest Road, Beacon Road, Park Road,
2.2 to 2.5 km south), and route 1 uses all three; a second route through them would repeat route 1's scenarios. The
named roundabouts to the west ("Warwick Way roundabout", "Derby Road roundabout") are recorded 300 m and more from any
roundabout, so they cannot be matched; the likely ones, Warwick Way Roundabout and Bishop Meadow Roundabout on Derby
Road, are 2 km and 1.8 km west, and Bishop Meadow is signal-controlled on its ring. With better positions in the
evidence file (the junction nodes picked), route 2 could take the Warwick Way roundabout instead of the Bishop Meadow
Road mini-roundabout; that is for the next review.
