# Isleworth (Fleming Way) decision routes

Two "Decisions only" practice routes from the Isleworth test centre at Unit 8, The Wireless Factory, Fleming Way, TW7
6DB (DVSA's move bulletin of 16 April 2018; gov.uk not reachable from the build machine, *to recheck*). Built from
OpenStreetMap data and the published evidence in `content/sources/pass2/isleworth-fleming-way-evidence.json`,
November 2026. Practice routes, not confirmed test routes. How they were chosen and built: `HOW_WE_BUILT_IT.md`. The
format: `../ROUTE_FORMAT.md`.

**Start and finish.** Fleming Way is a service road south off Worton Road, 280 m west of the Twickenham Road lights;
its branch west runs past the units. Both routes start on it 35 m short of Worton Road, facing it, and finish back on
the branch by the units. Route 1 leaves right and comes back from the west (a right turn in); route 2 leaves left and
comes back from the east (a left turn in). *To confirm: where tests start and finish within Fleming Way.*

**Evidence.** Every road and junction in the evidence file is *likely* (one author; the second site copies it), and the
lists probably predate the 2018 move from Worton Hall, so how the routes leave and return rests on the map. The
overlap numbers below count those likely roads.

## Route 1: Twickenham Road, Mogden Lane and Whitton Dene (scene `isleworth-fleming-way-route-1`, 4.5 km)

Out of Fleming Way, right on to Worton Road, right at the Twickenham Road lights, Twickenham Road, right at the Mogden
Lane lights, Mogden Lane, ahead at the Mogden Lane roundabout on to Whitton Dene (pulling up and moving off), right at
the Hall Road mini-roundabout, Hall Road, Worton Road through the Bridge Road and Ruskin Road mini-roundabouts, right
into Fleming Way.

The mix: lights (twice), a large roundabout and a mini-roundabout, emerging right, a right turn across oncoming traffic
(3 and 8), and the pull-up and move-off pair. **No emerging left and no pedestrian crossing on this route** (route 2 has
both): the zebra by Lynton Close and the pelican on Twickenham Road are not built in the scene (see below), and the
route's only side-road exit is the test centre's. Known junctions used (3 of 8): the Worton Road / Twickenham Road
lights (2), the Mogden Lane roundabout (the evidence's "Whitton Dene / Rugby Road roundabout", 4) and the Hall Road /
Whitton Dene mini-roundabout (7).

**Overlap** (rule 3): 4.3 km of 4.5 km (95%) on likely roads (Worton Road 1.3 km, Hall Road 0.84, Twickenham Road 0.81,
Whitton Dene 0.72, Mogden Lane 0.58). Not named: Fleming Way and the roundabout's ring.

## Route 2: Bridge Road, London Road and St John's Road (scene `isleworth-fleming-way-route-2`, 4.0 km)

Out of Fleming Way, left on to Worton Road, through the Ruskin Road mini-roundabout, right at the Bridge Road
mini-roundabout, Bridge Road, right at the London Road lights, London Road over the zebra by Thornbury Road, right at
the St John's Road lights, St John's Road (pulling up and moving off), right at the Twickenham Road mini-roundabout,
Twickenham Road, right at the Worton Road lights, Worton Road, left into Fleming Way.

| 6 | Pull up on St John's Road, past St John's Court | A grey car behind | Choose short of Nottingham Road, mirrors, signal; handbrake, neutral, cancel signal |
| 7 | Move off | The grey car coming past, turning into Nottingham Road | Mirrors, wait; mirror and blind spot, go |
| 8 | Twickenham Road at the Worton Road lights, right into Worton Road | A white van coming up Twickenham Road | Mirrors, signal right, position; wait for it; look into Worton Road, go |

The mix: lights (3 and 5), a mini-roundabout, emerging left, a pedestrian crossing (the zebra; 3 is a crossing at
lights), right turns across oncoming traffic (5 and 8), and the pull-up and move-off pair. **No large roundabout and no
emerging right on this route** (route 1 has both): north and west of the centre the roundabouts are mini-roundabouts,
and the turns out of side roads on the way are at lights. Known junctions used (3 of 8): the Worton Road / Bridge Road
mini-roundabout (2), the London Road / Bridge Road lights (3) and the Worton Road / Twickenham Road lights (8, from the
Twickenham Road arm: route 1 takes it from Worton Road, a different decision).

**Overlap**: 2.95 km of 4.0 km (74%) on likely roads (Worton Road 1.05 km, London Road 0.88, Bridge Road 0.65,
Twickenham Road 0.36). Not named: St John's Road (0.91 km) and Fleming Way.

**The two routes together**: they leave in different directions (route 1 right and east, route 2 left and west) and
share Worton Road between the test centre and the Twickenham Road lights, which every route needs. 16 different
challenges. Of the known junctions in reach that suit a scenario, five are used; the A4 lights, the A316 Chertsey Road
roundabouts and the Spring Grove Road mini-roundabouts are further out than a 4 km route reaches, and the A316 is
dual carriageway trunk road.

## Found on the map

- **Twickenham Road is mapped in many short pieces** (several under 10 m). The scene builder drops a crossing within
  8 m of a piece's end, so the Lynton Close zebra and the pelican south of Worton Road are not built, and the signals on
  Twickenham Road's arms at the Worton Road lights are not either: in the scene those lights stop only Worton Road and
  Van Gogh Close. Route 2's last challenge says green, as it would be for the car.
- **Whitton Dene at the Mogden Lane roundabout** began with a 6.5 m piece the lane path could not use: the car's line
  cut across the island. Two nodes were moved west along the road in `osm-patches.json`.
- **The Mogden Lane roundabout's entry from Mogden Lane** has a bollard on its splitter island's nose close to the
  car's line: route 1 keeps 0.9 m further left on the way in (a `shift`).
- **The left turn into Fleming Way** is tight: route 2 keeps 0.7 m out from the kerb (a `shift`).
- **Woodlands Road and Riverside Walk** end in cycle paths, so there is no way back to Worton Road from St John's
  Road except by Twickenham Road.
