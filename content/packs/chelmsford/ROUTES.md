# Chelmsford decision routes

Two "Decisions only" practice routes from the Chelmsford test centre at Unit 12, Cromar Way, CM1 2GL (car tests moved
there from Hanbury Road on 5 October 2026: DVSA notice published by the Motor Schools Association, 2 October 2026).
Built from OpenStreetMap data, November 2026. Practice routes, not confirmed test routes. How they were chosen and
built: `HOW_WE_BUILT_IT.md`. The format: `../ROUTE_FORMAT.md`.

**Start and finish.** Cromar Way is a dead end off Waterhouse Lane, with traffic lights where it meets it, so both
routes leave and come back that way. The map has no gate for Unit 12 itself, so the drive starts on Cromar Way outside
units 12 to 13 and finishes back on Cromar Way short of them. *To confirm: the centre's own way in and out.*

## Route 1: south and Moulsham (scene `chelmsford-route-1`, 5.7 km)

Cromar Way, left on to Waterhouse Lane, Westway, the Britvic Roundabout, London Road, the Miami Roundabout, Moulsham
Street, right into St John's Road, Mildmay Road, Hamlet Road (pulling up and moving off), left down Moulsham Street, Southborough Road, New London
Road, Writtle Road, Westway, Waterhouse Lane, right into Cromar Way.

| # | Challenge | Road users | Right answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cromar Way lights, turn left | Red light; a pedestrian still crossing the end of Cromar Way as it turns green | Mirrors, signal left, stop; on green, give way until they are across; left mirror, go |
| 2 | Writtle Road lights on Waterhouse Lane, ahead | Red as you approach | Interior mirror, slow down, stop; on green, look across the junction, go |
| 3 | Britvic Roundabout, first exit | A white car coming round from the Widford arm | Mirrors, signal left; give way; look right again, go |
| 4 | Moulsham Street, right into St John's Road at the crossroads | A blue car coming down the one-way part of Moulsham Street | Mirrors, signal right, position; wait for it; look into St John's Road, go |
| 5 | Pull up on Hamlet Road, past Lady Lane | | Choose well past Lady Lane, mirrors, signal; handbrake, neutral, cancel signal |
| 6 | Move off | A cyclist coming up Hamlet Road behind, turning into Upper Roman Road | Mirror and blind spot, wait; again, go |
| 7 | Hamlet Road, left on to Moulsham Street (one-way, from the right) | A grey car coming down Moulsham Street | Mirrors, signal left; look right, give way; look right and left, go |
| 8 | Moulsham Street zebra crossing at St John's Road | A pedestrian stepping on from the left | Interior mirror, slow down; stop and give way; look both ways, go |

Found on the map: Moulsham Street north of St John's Road is **one-way southbound**, so the loop goes up it only as
far as the crossroads and comes back down it from Hamlet Road. Hamlet Road has a STOP line at its Lady Lane end (not
on this route: the route enters Hamlet Road from Mildmay Road). St John's Road has cars parked along its far kerb every
25 m or so: no room for anyone to pass a car pulled up there, so the pull-up is on Hamlet Road.

## Route 2: north by Rainsford and Melbourne (scene `chelmsford-route-2`, 7.6 km)

Cromar Way, right on to Waterhouse Lane, Rainsford Lane, straight over the Parkway lights into Primrose Hill, left on
to Rainsford Road, Roxwell Road, right at the Chignal Road lights, right into Melbourne Avenue, the North Avenue
roundabout, North Avenue, right on to Kings Road, Swiss Avenue, Park Avenue, left on to Rainsford Road, Parkway,
Rainsford Lane, Waterhouse Lane, left into Cromar Way.

| # | Challenge | Road users | Right answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cromar Way lights, turn right | Red light | Mirrors, signal right, stop; on green, look both ways along Waterhouse Lane, go |
| 2 | Primrose Hill, left on to Rainsford Road | Someone crossing the end of Primrose Hill; then a green car coming along Rainsford Road from the right | Mirrors, signal left, give way to the pedestrian; look right, give way; look right and left, go |
| 3 | Chignal Road lights, turn right | Green; a blue car coming the other way along Roxwell Road | Mirrors, signal right, position; wait in the junction; look into Chignal Road, go |
| 4 | Pull up on Melbourne Avenue, past Pines Road | A grey car behind | Choose past Pines Road, mirrors, signal; handbrake, neutral, cancel signal |
| 5 | Move off | The grey car coming past | Mirrors, wait; mirror and blind spot, go |
| 6 | Melbourne Avenue zebra crossing by Salerno Way | A pedestrian stepping on from the left | Interior mirror, slow down; stop and give way; look both ways, go |
| 7 | North Avenue roundabout, turn right (second exit) | A white car coming up North Avenue and round from the right | Mirrors, signal right; give way; look right again, go |
| 8 | North Avenue, right on to Kings Road | A black car coming along Kings Road from the right | Mirrors, signal right; look right, give way; look right, left and right, go |

Found on the map: from Rainsford Lane, Rainsford Road westbound is reached by Primrose Hill (one-way), straight over
the Parkway lights; North Avenue is a one-way pair with small roundabouts.
