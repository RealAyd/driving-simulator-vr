# Hendon (London) decision routes

Two "Decisions only" practice routes from the Hendon test centre at 3 Aviation Drive, Beaufort Park, NW9 5TZ (the
evidence file's address, checked 15 November 2026; the map's "DSA Driving Test Centre" point is on Aviation Drive, off
the Boulevard Drive / Caversham Road crossroads). Built from OpenStreetMap data and the published evidence in
`content/sources/pass2/hendon-london-evidence.json`, November 2026. Practice routes, not confirmed test routes. How
they were chosen and built: `HOW_WE_BUILT_IT.md`. The format: `../ROUTE_FORMAT.md`.

**Start and finish.** Both routes start on Aviation Drive facing the crossroads and leave down Caversham Road to
Aerodrome Road, as every route in the old DVSA list leaves ("left" or "right" on to Aerodrome Road). Route 1 comes
back by Heritage Avenue and Boulevard Drive, route 2 by Caversham Road; both finish on Aviation Drive by the centre.
*To confirm: that Caversham Road is the way tests leave today.*

## Route 1: Greyhound Hill, The Burroughs and Watford Way (scene `hendon-london-route-1`, 4.5 km)

Out of the centre, Caversham Road, left on to Aerodrome Road, ahead at the Watford Way lights into Greyhound Hill,
over its zebra, Church End, right on to The Burroughs (pulling up and moving off), Watford Way north (by Station Road),
left at the lights into Aerodrome Road, right at the Grahame Park Way roundabout, Grahame Park Way, right into
Heritage Avenue, Boulevard Drive and back up Aviation Drive.

The mix: lights (twice, ahead and left), a large roundabout, emerging left and right, a zebra, and the pull-up and
move-off pair. **No mini-roundabout** (route 2 has the Heywood Avenue one) and **no right turn across oncoming
traffic** (below). Known junctions: the Aerodrome Road / Watford Way lights (2, 7) and the Grahame Park Way roundabout
(8).

**Overlap** (rule 3): 80% of 4.5 km on roads in the evidence file (Aerodrome Road 1.4 km, Watford Way 0.9 km, The
Burroughs 0.5 km, Greyhound Hill 0.4 km, Grahame Park Way, Church End). 3 of 8 challenges at known junctions.

## Route 2: Colindale, Booth Road and Lanacre Avenue (scene `hendon-london-route-2`, 4.1 km)

Avenue and right into Booth Road (pulling up and moving off), right on to Montrose Avenue, right at the Montrose Avenue
mini-roundabout into Lanacre Avenue, ahead at the Heywood Avenue mini-roundabout, right at the Quakers Course
mini-roundabout down Lanacre Avenue and Bristol Avenue over its zebra, Grahame Park Way, left at the roundabout on to

The mix: a large roundabout (twice, from different arms), a mini-roundabout, emerging right (twice), a zebra, and the
pull-up and move-off pair. **No lights and no emerging left** (route 1 has both). Known junctions: the Grahame Park
Way roundabout (2, 8) and the Lanacre Avenue mini-roundabout (6).

**Overlap**: 82% of 4.1 km on roads in the evidence file (Aerodrome Road 1.0 km, Lanacre Avenue 1.0 km, Booth Road
0.9 km, Montrose Avenue, Grahame Park Way). Not named anywhere: Bristol Avenue (0.25 km, Lanacre Avenue's way down to
Grahame Park Way), Caversham Road and Aviation Drive (the centre's way out). 3 of 8 challenges at known junctions.

**The two routes together**: they leave in different directions (route 1 east to Watford Way, route 2 west to
Colindale and north into Grahame Park) and share Caversham Road and Aerodrome Road west of it. 16 different
scenarios; the Grahame Park Way roundabout is used three times, each from a different arm or to a different exit. Of
the known junctions that suit a scenario and lie within reach, three of the four are used (the Aerodrome Road /
Watford Way lights, the Grahame Park Way roundabout, the Lanacre Avenue mini-roundabouts); Five Ways Corner is not
(below).

## Found on the map

- **No right turn across oncoming traffic.** The right turns near the centre are from Watford Way's dual carriageway
  (not offered from Aerodrome Road southbound; the list never uses one), at the Station Road junction (a set of
  slips, driven by Station Road) or 60 m past a roundabout (Colindale Avenue into Booth Road, too close to the
  roundabout to stand as its own challenge). Each route's right turns are driven, not set as challenges; a third
  route would be the place for one (Heritage Avenue from Aerodrome Road).
- **Five Ways Corner** (A1/A41) is a signal-controlled gyratory of one-way pieces: the engine does not see it as a
  roundabout. Left out, with the A1 beyond it.
- **The Aerodrome Road / Watford Way lights**: the link across the central reservation is a short unnamed way; it was
  named Aerodrome Road in the downloaded data (`osm-patches.json`) so the planner can go ahead into Greyhound Hill.
- **Bristol Avenue's link on to Grahame Park Way** is mapped as one lane beside a contraflow bus lane; drawn 3.2 m
  wide, the turn out of it crossed the kerb. Given its two lanes in the downloaded data.
- **The evidence file's junction positions** were the middle of a named road; the two this centre's routes use were
  set to the junctions themselves.
- **Church End / The Burroughs**: whether Church End gives way to The Burroughs is taken from the map's layout (a side
  road on to a primary road); not surveyed.
