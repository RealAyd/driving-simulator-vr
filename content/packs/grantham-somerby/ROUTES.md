# Grantham (Somerby) decision routes

Two "Decisions only" practice routes from the Grantham (Somerby) test centre at Spitalgate Airfield, Blue Harbour,
Grantham NG31 7TX (evidence file, 15 November 2026: gov.uk's centre finder could not be read automatically; a 2014
gov.uk impact assessment and an instructor's page give the move to the former goods vehicle testing station). Built from
OpenStreetMap data and the published evidence in `content/sources/pass2/grantham-somerby-evidence.json`, November 2026.
Practice routes, not confirmed test routes. How they were chosen and built: `HOW_WE_BUILT_IT.md`. The format:
`../ROUTE_FORMAT.md`.

**Start and finish.** The map has no driving test centre point. At the evidence's position, the A52 (High Dike) bends
east into Bridge End Road where the B6403 (High Dike, Ermine Street) leaves north; 200 m south, on the west side of
the A52, is a site mapped as "Vehicle testing Station" with a short way in and a one-way loop round its yard. Both
routes start and finish on that way in. *To confirm: that this is the centre's entrance, and which way tests turn out
of it.*

The town, and every road the evidence names, is 2.5 to 5 km west of the centre, over the hill (Somerby Hill) or round
by Harrowby Lane; the centre's own roads (High Dike, the A52 and the B6403) are 60 mph roads through fields. So both
routes are long (10 to 11 km): out on the 60 mph roads, a loop on the town's east side, and back.

## Route 1: High Dike and Harrowby Lane (scene `grantham-somerby-route-1`, 11.1 km)

Out of the centre, left on to High Dike (A52), left on to High Dike (B6403), left into Harrowby Lane, along it past
Alma Park Road (pulling up and moving off), left at the New Beacon Road mini-roundabout into Uplands Drive, Tennyson
Avenue and Keats Avenue, right on to Harrowby Lane, back to the B6403, right on to it, and right into the test centre.

The mix: a mini-roundabout, emerging left and right (twice: a 30 mph estate road and a 60 mph road), a right turn across
oncoming traffic, the pull-up and move-off pair, and a left turn behind a cyclist on a 60 mph road. **No traffic
lights, large roundabout or pedestrian crossing** on this route: the area has no lights east of Harrowby Road; route 2
has the Somerby Hill roundabout, the Sandon Road lights and a zebra. Harrowby Lane's zebra is 230 m from the
mini-roundabout, too close for both to be challenges (the mini-roundabout is the only one in reach, so it was kept).

**Overlap** (rule 3): the checker gives 55% on confirmed or likely roads (Harrowby Lane 5.7 km both ways, Alma Park Road
and Kenilworth Road at their mouths, the A52's Bridge End Road at the centre). Not counted: High Dike (3.7 km, the A52
and the B6403 to and from the centre; the route list names "High Dyke", which the evidence file left unmatched), and
Uplands Drive, Tennyson Avenue and Keats Avenue (0.98 km, the loop round from the mini-roundabout). Counting High Dike
as the route list names it: 90%.

**Known junctions: 1 of 8 by the checker, NOT MET (the area cannot meet it; see below).** The checker counts challenge
6 (Keats Avenue), 60 m from the point the evidence gives for the "Harrowby Lane roundabout"; that point is a position
on Harrowby Lane, not a junction (the file says so). The roundabout the route list means is, on the map, the New
Beacon Road mini-roundabout on Harrowby Lane, the only one on it: challenge 5 (0.5 km from the evidence's point).

## Route 2: Somerby Hill and Harrowby Road (scene `grantham-somerby-route-2`, 10.3 km)

Out of the centre, right on to High Dike (A52), right at the Somerby Hill roundabout on to Somerby Hill (pulling up and
moving off on the hill), Bridge End Road, right into Harrowby Road, ahead at the Sandon Road lights, right from Sandon
Road on to Hill Avenue, New Beacon Road, Beacon Lane, left at the lights back down Harrowby Road over its zebra, left on
to Bridge End Road, and home over Somerby Hill, the roundabout and High Dike.

The mix: lights, a large roundabout, emerging right (twice), a right turn across oncoming traffic, a zebra, and the
pull-up and move-off pair. **No mini-roundabout and no emerging left** on this route (route 1 has both; the Harrowby
Road / Bridge End Road emerge left on the way home is 200 m after the zebra, too close to be a challenge too).

**Overlap**: the checker gives **44%, NOT MET**, but the route is 85% on roads the evidence confirms or names. The
difference is Somerby Hill (5.3 km, both ways): the evidence records it as confirmed ("A52 Somerby Hill", two
sources), but with one OpenStreetMap way (228700677, the 30 mph end by Bridge End Road) and a name the checker does not
match to the map's "Somerby Hill" (its name match keeps "a52"), so only 1.1 km of it counts. Counted on the evidence:
Somerby Hill 5.3 km, Harrowby Road 1.6, Sandon Road 0.8, New Beacon Road 0.5, Hill Avenue 0.4, Bridge End Road 0.25.
Not named anywhere: Beacon Lane (0.25 km, from New Beacon Road back to the lights) and the centre's way in; High Dike
(1.0 km) is the route list's unmatched "High Dyke" (95% counting it).

**Known junctions: 1 of 8 by the checker, NOT MET (the area cannot meet it).** Counted: the move-off, at the
evidence's "hill starts and stops on gradients near the centre" (Somerby Hill; the pull-up is 64 m from that point,
just outside the checker's 60 m). Challenge 6 is at the junction the route list calls the "Harrowby Road lights" (the
only lights on Harrowby Road), but the evidence's point for it is a position on Harrowby Road 365 m south. By what the
evidence names: 3 (pull-up, move-off, the Harrowby Road lights).

**The two routes together**: they leave in different directions (route 1 left and north up the B6403, route 2 right
and south to the Somerby Hill roundabout) and share only the centre's way in and the 200 m of the A52 to the B6403. 16
different scenarios.

## Why the known-junction rule cannot be met

The evidence's eight non-road places come from a route list for the old Cecil Street centre (seven) and one
instructor's page (the hill). Four are 3.5 to 5.5 km west, through the town centre: the Wharf Road and Barrowby Road
roundabouts, and the Belton Lane / Manthorpe Road and Watergate / Brook Street lights. A scene (3.3 km square here)
holding the centre cannot also hold them, and a route there and back would be 15 km or more, mostly on the A52. The
other three in reach are used, as above (the Harrowby Lane mini-roundabout, the Harrowby Road lights and the hill).
The A1 (confirmed) is a dual carriageway 5 km west: not used.

## Found on the map

- **The B6403 at Harrowby Lane** (route 1): the B6403's way runs on 1.5 km beyond the scene from a node 8 m past the
  junction, so the scene ended it there and the planner did not find the junction (side roads count from 8 m in). That
  node was moved 150 m on along the same straight line in the downloaded data (`osm-patches.json`).
- **The centre's way in** is unnamed and 35 m long: the planner takes such a road for a link and will not turn into
  it. It was named "Driving Test Centre" in the downloaded data (its street plate reads so).
- **The Sandon Road lights** (route 2): the scene puts the stop lines 10 m back from the junction node, beyond the
  signal crossings on Harrowby Road and Beacon Lane, so the car stops with its tail on the crossing. Not patched (the
  lights are mapped right); listed for the engine.
- **The Somerby Hill crossing** (an uncontrolled crossing on the map) is built as a zebra; the route passes it both
  ways without a challenge.
- The ground is flat in the simulator: the hill start on Somerby Hill is a pull-up and move-off on the hill's road, with
  the hill only in the words.
